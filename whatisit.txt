How Each Program Connects to That Model:


01_data_exploration.py
 (Data Discovery)
Inspects the raw dataset of 80 food categories, checks class balance, and confirms dimensions before training.



02_data_pipeline.py
 (Data Preparation)
Creates the stratified 70/15/15 train, validation, and test splits in 

configs/
 so the model trains reproducibly.



03_model_build_and_train.py
 (Base Model Training)
Builds the EfficientNetB0 transfer learning architecture (frozen backbone + classification head) and saves the initial checkpoint: 

best_model.keras
.



05_fine_tune.py
 (Final Model Creation - Your Ultimate Goal)
Loads the base model, unfreezes the top 30 layers of the EfficientNet backbone, trains at a lower learning rate ($10^{-5}$), and produces 

best_model_finetuned.keras
.



06_predict.py
 (Model Verification & Testing)
Loads that exact best_model_finetuned.keras and tests it on real images to confirm its accuracy and output top predictions.
